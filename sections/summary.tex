\cvsection{Summary}

\begin{cvparagraph}

\textcolor{black}{I am an Associate Professor of Public Administration at Universidad Diego Portales and a Postdoctoral Researcher in Computational Social Science at Leiden University. I hold a DPhil (PhD) in Politics from the University of Oxford. My work bridges the fields of comparative politics, government and computational social science. I build large-scale text-as-data pipelines, deploy AI and machine learning models, and apply causal inference strategies. My recent peer-reviewed articles have appeared in {\itshape Nature}, {\itshape Social Science Computer Review}, {\itshape Public Opinion Quarterly}, {\itshape World Development}, {\itshape Artificial Intelligence and Law}, among others. I regularly present at IPSA, ECPR, EPSA, and COMPTEXT. At ECPR, I serve on the Steering Committee of the Standing Group on Political Methodology.}

\textcolor{black}{I am currently working on the Terrorist Group Adaptation \& Lessons for Counterterrorism (TERGAP) project, funded by the European Union, to build a new dataset of counterterrorism events, which will help researchers identify patterns of adaptation, examine unintended effects of counterterrorism, and generate evidence to improve global security. In parallel, I contribute to COST Action CA22150 on executive-bureaucratic careers, and I serve as a Research Leader at the Oxford Computational Political Science Group.}
\vspace{1mm}
\end{cvparagraph}
